# Supplementary figure legends

**Figure S1. Weight grid search for the local case study (Case 1).** Mean cumulative ASV richness across 50 simulation runs for each of the 66 combinations of (W_rich, W_uniq, W_unc) on a simplex grid with step 0.1, evaluated at n = 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 samples (ternary panels; colour scale, mean richness). Red diamond, best combination at each depth; white circle with black outline, the combination used in the main analysis (0.4, 0.3, 0.3). Lower panel, trajectory of the best combination with increasing n. The simulations used the same engine (regression kriging), batch size and pilot sets (runs 1–50) as Fig. 2. Every combination that retained all three terms performed within 2% of the best.

**Figure S2. Weight grid search for the global case study (Case 2).** As Fig. S1, for the GlobalFungi subsample: 66 weight combinations × 10 simulation runs, Quantile Regression Forests with 100 trees (reduced from the 200 used in Fig. 3 to limit run time), evaluated at n = 1,200, 1,500, 2,000, 2,500 and 3,000 samples. White circle with black outline, the combination used in the main analysis (0.2, 0.6, 0.2). The best combinations at every depth place most of the weight on uniqueness, and the richness-only corner (W_rich = 1) is the worst combination at every depth.

**Figure S3. Spatial trajectory of a single BAS campaign in the local case study.** Maps of the 53 candidate sites in the forest headwater catchment (thin lines, elevation contours; thick outline, catchment boundary) after n = 5 (pilot), 10, 20 and 30 samples. Orange diamonds, sites added in the most recent batch; blue circles, sites sampled in earlier batches; black dots, unsampled candidates. Weights (0.4, 0.3, 0.3), regression kriging engine, batch size 5, pilot drawn with seed 42. An animated version showing every batch is provided as a GIF (FigS3_SpatialTrajectory_local.gif).

**Figure S4. Spatial trajectory of a single BAS campaign in the global case study.** Locations of the 5,000 GlobalFungi sites after n = 1,000 (pilot), 1,500, 2,000 and 3,000 samples; symbols as in Fig. S3. The pilot was drawn with a 20-fold preference for sites north of 20°N, and the first adaptive batches concentrate in South America, southern Africa, Australia and the tropics, the regions the pilot left unsampled (quantified in Fig. 4C). Weights (0.2, 0.6, 0.2), Quantile Regression Forest engine (200 trees), batch size 100, pilot drawn with seed 42. An animated version is provided as a GIF (FigS4_SpatialTrajectory_global.gif).
